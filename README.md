# Inventory Management System

A simple system that helps you keep track of your **Current Inventory**, plan the
**Things to Order**, makes **Purchase Tracking** easy, and lets you **Cross Verify**
the items received.

## Sections and Categories

Every business has different places that need their own items — like a
**Restaurant**, **Bar**, **Bakery**, **Tobacco Section**, and so on. Each of these
places is a **Section** in the app.

Inside a Section, items are grouped into **Categories** — for example, Beers,
Wines and Spirits in the Bar. This way, the people working in each place order
only what their place needs.

## What the app is used for

- **Current Inventory** — see at a glance what stock you have.
- **Things to Order** — each section notes what it is running low on and sends
  the list for purchase.
- **Purchase Tracking** — all orders land in one shopping list, and purchases
  are marked off as they are bought.
- **Cross Verify Received Items** — when a delivery arrives, check it against
  what was ordered. Received items automatically add to Current Inventory.
